## Full description

Amika is a private, low-pressure place to remember life with the people you love. Add a photo or a few words, tag the friend who was there, and choose exactly who can see it.

Build a friendship timeline from the tiny things: the joke from lunch, the song someone sent, the long walk, or the ordinary Tuesday you never want to lose. React and reply with your circle, message friends privately, and keep personal reflections in your journal.

Amika is made for memories—not followers, performance, or trip planning.
